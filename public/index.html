Which steel system fits my plans?
Site-built framing
Cold-formed studs and track form walls. Hot-rolled beams, columns and hollow sections form frames. Plans may combine them with concrete floors, masonry cores or timber.
Pre-engineered buildings
Price a complete metal building with rigid portal frames, secondary framing and cladding. Include clear height, openings, bay spacing and any crane loads.
Prefabrication
Panelized walls and roof assemblies arrive as flat sections. Volumetric modules arrive as three-dimensional units. Request freight, lifting and field connections separately.
Long spans & special structures
Compare open-web joists, trusses, space frames, steel arches and cable-supported roofs where called for on the plans. Identify braced frames, moment frames or plate shear walls in the engineer’s lateral design.

THE STEEL ADVANTAGE

Why build with steel?

Compare fire performance, dimensional stability, fabrication efficiency and seismic design for your project.

✦

Noncombustible framing

Steel studs do not add combustible fuel to a fire. The rating of the complete wall, floor or roof assembly still depends on tested components and detailing.

Fire-focused design & risk planning
◇

Termite-resistant structural framing

Galvanized steel studs, tracks and joists are not edible to termites. Unlike untreated wood framing, the steel structure itself cannot be consumed by these pests. Sheathing, trim and other non-steel components may still need protection.

Repair costs & strength loss

What repairs can cost. In a 2024 U.S. survey of 600 homeowners, 94% of those who reported termite damage paid under $3,000 for repairs. The remaining 6% paid more. Over a home’s life, costs depend on repeat infestations and which parts need replacement. The survey measured repair bills over an unspecified period. Homeowner survey ↗

What strength loss looks like. In tests of termite-exposed hybrid-pine beams, the researchers’ trend line showed about 40% lower bending strength at 30% wood mass loss compared with zero loss. Tunnel location mattered, and individual results varied. A whole-home assessment requires inspection of the affected members and connections. Beam study, figure 10 ↗

Lower termite-related framing damage exposure
↔

Stays straight and stable

Cold-formed steel framing doesn't warp, shrink or twist as lumber can with moisture fluctuations, helping maintain alignment of walls and finishes.

Less framing-related rework risk
◆

Galvanized corrosion protection

Zinc-coated framing resists corrosion when properly specified and detailed. Coating class, moisture exposure and coastal conditions must be reviewed.

Service-life durability by design
⏱

Faster framing with prefabrication

Pre-cut, labeled steel panels and trusses reduce on-site measuring and cutting. A 233-unit Michigan apartment project reported 30% faster framing versus conventional methods; this is a project result, not a universal promise.

Potentially fewer framing weeks and field labor hours
♻

Recyclable and documentable

Steel can be recycled repeatedly. Recycled content and embodied impacts vary by supplier; request mill certificates, recycled-content evidence and EPDs.

Traceable sustainability procurement

Built for California earthquakes.
Engineered for greater resilience.

Properly designed cold-formed steel structural systems can resist earthquake forces using engineered shear walls, bracing, connections, hold-downs and a continuous load path to the foundation. KNOW BEFORE YOU QUOTE

Common questions,
straight answers.

Material specs, pricing and engineering are different for every building. The job of this network is to make those details clear.

How much construction time can steel framing save?

It depends on the design and whether the framing arrives as precision-cut panels or is assembled piece by piece. In one 233-unit Michigan apartment project, the team reported 30% faster framing than conventional methods. In a separate English housing development, 20–30% shorter construction time was reported. Neither result is guaranteed for your project. We recommend requesting both a steel and a wood schedule with the same scope. Michigan case study ↗

Is galvanized steel actually resistant to termites?

Yes. Termites cannot eat steel studs, tracks, trusses or other steel framing because steel provides no food source. This does not make the entire property termite-proof: wood trim, sheathing and furnishings may still be vulnerable, and local pest-protection rules still apply. Industry source ↗

Does steel framing exceed California earthquake requirements?

It can, when an engineer designs the complete system—including shear walls, connections and foundations—for the site. Steel alone does not guarantee superior seismic performance. California’s current code references cold-formed steel seismic design standards. California Building Standards Commission ↗

Can you also connect us with installers?

Yes. Builders can request licensed installation contractors and share plans with invited firms by consent. Installation estimates show labor, equipment, mobilization and other scope costs. Contractors must be independently verified for the project location, license classification, workers’ compensation and insurance. The builder and installation contractor negotiate and sign their own construction agreement.

Does Gauge Better Network charge builders and suppliers?

Gauge Better Network may charge for separate builder sourcing and comparison services and may receive supplier-paid marketing or referral compensation under separately negotiated agreements. Any applicable charges and financial relationships are disclosed to the parties before they agree. No universal commission rate is published. Installation-related referral compensation is subject to applicable law and legal review.

How does Gauge Better Network connect me with materials suppliers?

Share your plans, takeoff or material list. Gauge Better Network connects you with independent manufacturers, distributors and fabricators for quotes and availability. You choose a supplier and arrange pricing, orders and delivery directly with that company.

Are estimates free?

Submitting a project or materials request is free. Each project may have an optional owner-set fee to unlock its approved supplier comparisons, displayed before payment. Fees do not guarantee a minimum number of bids or a lower price.

Can suppliers see one another's prices?

Only if the project owner enables an anonymized comparison board, the relevant quotes are approved, the supplier opts in, and estimate access has been unlocked. Supplier contact details and proprietary notes are not shown to competing suppliers.

Will steel always be cheaper than wood?

No. Total installed cost varies by steel design, labor familiarity, engineering, fire and thermal requirements, fabrication, order size, freight and local material prices. Compare equal scopes to identify the better value.

Does galvanized steel mean the whole building is fireproof or rustproof?

No. Steel framing is noncombustible and zinc coatings provide corrosion resistance, but complete assemblies need fire ratings and moisture/thermal engineering. Steel does not make a building immune to fire or corrosion.

Can you quote an ADU or a full commercial building?

Yes, request a project-specific assessment. We'll distinguish framing packages, nonstructural components, exterior wall systems and primary structural steel. Engineering and availability are confirmed for each application.
