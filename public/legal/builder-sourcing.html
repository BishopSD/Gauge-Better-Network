What a builder receives

Intake of project requirements and permitted plans; identification of potentially suitable steel suppliers; coordination of independent bid submissions; summary comparison of scope, timing, freight and price; optional connections to licensed installation firms.

Optional builder-side compensation

The builder may separately agree to pay Gauge Better Network for sourcing, project coordination or estimate access. Any service fee, computation basis, when it becomes due, any cancellation terms, and the exact services included should be agreed in writing before work or payment.

Disclosure of supplier compensation

Gauge Better Network may also be compensated by a supplier for distinct marketing or introduction services. Any material dual-sided compensation relationship should be clearly disclosed to the parties in the individual written agreements. No preset percentage is advertised.

What is not included

Gauge Better Network does not provide engineering approvals, licensed construction work, structural design, building inspections, guaranteed prices, legal advice, contractor license assurances, or ownership of building materials.

Suggested signed schedule

Record project ID; builder legal name; project scope; authorized plans; deliverables; service fee basis, amount and due date; refund triggers; disclosure of relationships; consent to sharing; confidentiality; signature and date. Use a lawyer-approved agreement before taking fees.
