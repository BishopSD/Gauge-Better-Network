Compliance & Transparency

Review date: November 12, 2026. This is a compliance status notice, not a government approval or legal certification.

Our commitment

Gauge Better Network is committed to applicable California and federal requirements for privacy, transparent service fees and contractor introductions. Independent suppliers and appropriately licensed contractors contract directly with customers; project engineering, permits and code compliance remain project-specific.

Current site status

Current status: preview only. Online submissions, uploads and payments are unavailable. Operational verification and final legal review remain required before public transactions.

Contractor introductions

Customers select, negotiate with, contract with and pay independent contractors directly. Verify the appropriate active CSLB license, classification, insurance and workers’ compensation before engagement. GBN does not undertake construction, negotiate construction contracts or receive construction payments. Any installation referral compensation requires review for the specific arrangement before use.

Transparent fees

Project submissions are intended to be free. Any GBN comparison-access or separately agreed sourcing fee must be disclosed before checkout, together with scope, cancellation and refund terms and relevant financial relationships. No checkout is active in this preview.

Before accepting online transactions

Confirm the registered operator name, business registration and applicable tax obligations; finalize service, refund and privacy terms; assess applicable California privacy duties; connect and test secure backend storage, authorization, deletion and payment verification; verify partner credentials and lawful referral arrangements; and review accessibility with keyboard and assistive technology. These checks have not all been completed.

Questions or accessibility assistance

Call (951) 201-8809 for privacy, service or accessibility assistance. No ADA, WCAG, CCPA/CPRA or building-code certification is asserted.
