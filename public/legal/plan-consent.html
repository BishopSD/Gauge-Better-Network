Purpose

Construction drawings supplied through Gauge Better Network are collected only for project review, steel-material sourcing, bid preparation and, when separately enabled by the builder, installation-quote preparation.

Default supplier sharing

By selecting the project submission consent box, you authorize Gauge Better Network staff and the specific suppliers its owner invites to view relevant plan files for quotation. Plans are never part of the public catalog.

Separate installer sharing

You choose whether invited independent installer firms can see plan files. Switching installer plan access off prevents future downloads via that project’s installer links, though it cannot recall copies already downloaded.

Security of access links

Builders receive private project keys. Supplier and installer links are scoped to one project, intended for a specific business email, time-limited and owner-revocable. These are bearer links: a forwarded link may be accessed by another person. Do not share the link or upload prohibited documents.

Licenses and intellectual property

You confirm that you own the drawings or have permission to supply them for estimate purposes. You retain rights to your drawings. The portal does not transfer architectural copyright to Gauge Better Network or the quoting partner.

Removal and retention

Contact the owner at (951) 201-8809 to request revocation of invitation access or review of document deletion. A verified deletion workflow and retention schedule must be finalized prior to public launch.

Compliance & transparency

Gauge Better Network is committed to applicable California and federal requirements for privacy, transparent service fees and contractor introductions. Independent suppliers and appropriately licensed contractors contract directly with customers; project engineering, permits and code compliance remain project-specific.
