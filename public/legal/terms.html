Scope of service

Gauge Better Network helps business users request galvanized steel sourcing estimates, compare independent supplier offers, and request introductions to installation firms. Gauge Better Network does not own, sell, stock, fabricate, deliver or install steel and is not a construction contractor, engineering firm, employer or insurer.

No guaranteed results

Project applications, matches, estimates and referrals do not guarantee product suitability, permits, code compliance, availability, price, schedule, contractor credentials, or completion of a sale.

Estimates and payments

Submitting a project is free unless a separate service has been agreed. Approved bid comparisons may carry a project-specific fee shown before checkout; no payment should be required before at least one reviewed bid is available. Separate builder sourcing services and supplier services may carry individually negotiated compensation, documented separately before invoicing.

Independent suppliers and installers

Suppliers set material prices, terms, warranties and fulfillment. Independent licensed construction firms provide their own installation estimates and contract directly with builders. Gauge Better Network does not supervise jobsite work, negotiate construction contracts, or hold installer payments.

Plans, permissions and confidentiality

Upload only plans you have permission to use. You authorize limited review and sharing with individually invited suppliers for the purpose of preparing estimates, and with installers only when you separately opt in. Treat invitation links and project links as confidential. Do not upload restricted or third-party plans without required permissions.

Technical comparisons

Steel framing may offer termite resistance, dimensional stability, noncombustible members, and prefabrication opportunities. Engineering, building codes, insulation, corrosion protection, labor and project design determine actual results. Promotional case-study percentages are not project-specific warranties.

Disputes and applicable rules

Fees, cancellations, refunds, liability, dispute resolution, governing law, accessibility and notice contacts must be finalized by counsel before these terms are relied on for public transactions.
