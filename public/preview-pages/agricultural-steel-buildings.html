AGRICULTURAL CONSTRUCTION

Steel framing options for working agricultural spaces.

Agricultural buildings can expose framing materials to pests, moisture, washing operations and equipment impacts. Galvanized steel components offer termite resistance and a range of specified coating and framing options.

Barns and equipment shelters

Consider the intended use, clear openings, roof and wind requirements, vehicle clearances and any framing needed for cladding or insulation.

Exposure and coating selection

Fertilizers, animal environments and coastal conditions can create aggressive exposure. Share those conditions so suppliers can quote suitable specified coatings and details.

Purpose-built supply packages

A request may cover studs, tracks, framing kits, purlins, girts, connectors, roofing or wall cladding. Suppliers can identify which products are compatible with your engineered building system.
