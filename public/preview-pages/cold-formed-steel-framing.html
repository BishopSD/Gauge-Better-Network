FRAMING TECHNOLOGY

Precision framing. Smarter installation planning.

Cold-formed steel (CFS) uses shaped steel members for light-gauge structural and nonstructural applications. Its consistent profiles and factory-fabricated panel options can help crews organize installation sequences and reduce field cutting.

Component accuracy

Steel studs and tracks are manufactured to defined dimensions. That consistency supports repeatable wall layouts, openings, service coordination, and predictable fastening locations.

Prefabrication possibilities

Depending on supplier capability, wall and roof assemblies can be prepared off-site, labeled for installation, and scheduled for delivery by building area. Panelization can shorten on-site framing activity when design, logistics and crews are coordinated.

How to request a meaningful schedule

Ask suppliers whether they offer cut-to-length members, engineered framing layouts, shop-fabricated wall panels, sequencing, and installation documentation. Compare lead time separately from field erection time.

BEFORE REQUESTING A QUOTE

Prepare a clear project scope.

  • Wall and roof framing plans
  • Prefabricated vs. stick-built preference
  • Crane or delivery access
  • Installation sequencing requirements
