INDUSTRIAL CONSTRUCTION

Steel components for industrial building programs.

Industrial facilities often use a mix of structural systems, exterior walls, interior partitions, roof secondary framing and specialty assemblies. Gauge Better Network helps teams source applicable galvanized steel components.

Submit plans & request estimates ↗Explore materials →

Warehouse and distribution spaces

Common sourcing requests include secondary framing, partitions, cladding supports, roof components and compatible packaged systems. The required spans and loads determine the structural solution.

Manufacturing and flex spaces

Projects may have elevated service demands, fire separation requirements, equipment access and changing tenant layouts. Materials requests should identify which walls or supports are engineered or non-loadbearing.

Procurement at scale

Industrial bids benefit from unitized quantities, specifications, lead times, shipping destinations and phased fulfillment details, especially for large or multi-site programs.

BEFORE REQUESTING A QUOTE

Prepare a clear project scope.

  • Building drawings and framing scope
  • Specified loads and member schedules
  • Facility access and delivery constraints
  • Procurement phases and target dates
