A precise framing option for homes of every scale.

From custom houses to guesthouses, galvanized steel framing can provide dimensionally consistent wall, roof and floor components. Builders can evaluate prefabrication opportunities alongside the design and finishing requirements of the home.

Custom homes

Steel framing can support tailored layouts and coordinated openings. Contractors can compare supplier capabilities for engineered framing, roof trusses, connections and material delivery.

Garages, workshops and additions

Smaller projects still benefit from accurate materials lists and accessible component options. The project scope should include attachment details where new steel framing meets existing structures.

Plan for a complete assembly

Residential bids should identify wall and roof assemblies, insulation interfaces, service penetrations, corrosion/coating exposure, and installation scope. These details help align builder expectations with the supplied materials.
