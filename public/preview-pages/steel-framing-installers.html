INSTALLATION CONNECTIONS

Connect the steel package with the right installation team.

Even a well-designed steel package depends on skilled assembly. Gauge Better Network helps builders submit installation requirements and request introductions to relevant, independently licensed construction firms.

Submit plans & request estimates ↗Explore materials →

Experience that matches the system

Different scopes call for different skills: CFS wall framing, factory panel erection, roof and floor systems, bracing connections, cladding interfaces or structural steel work. Include those details in the request.

Compare installation bids separately

A useful labor proposal lists scope, crew availability, equipment needs, mobilization, site conditions, duration and exclusions. Comparing those items alongside material delivery can help coordinate the schedule.

Credentials and project fit

Before selecting an installer, confirm the contractor’s license classification, status, insurance, workers’ compensation and project references. The licensed firm provides its own contracting terms.

BEFORE REQUESTING A QUOTE

Prepare a clear project scope.

  • Installation scope and trade requirements
  • Plans authorized for installer review
  • Site access and schedule
  • Project city and preferred start
