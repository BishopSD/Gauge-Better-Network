MATERIAL SOURCING

Galvanized steel framing, sourced around your project.

Steel framing is used in walls, floors, roofs, partitions, and prefabricated building components. Gauge Better Network helps construction teams organize their requirements and reach suitable manufacturers and distributors for project-specific pricing.

What builders can source

Cold-formed steel studs and tracks, floor joists, roof framing, bracing, connectors, prefabricated panels and compatible frame packages. Product selection is based on your drawings, specified member sizes, coating needs, and the manufacturer’s available systems.

A clearer way to compare

A useful steel quote separates materials, fabrication, delivery, lead time, included accessories, and exclusions. Providing the same drawings to approved suppliers lets you review more comparable proposals.

Best information to send

Supply architectural and structural drawings, project city, framing scope, requested gauges or thicknesses, delivery conditions, and your desired start date. A preliminary material list is useful when plans are still in development.

BEFORE REQUESTING A QUOTE

Prepare a clear project scope.

  • Architectural or structural plans
  • Desired steel component types
  • Jobsite delivery location
  • Target delivery date
